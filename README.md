# Partner Ecosystem Builder

A Claude skill for partner ecosystem strategy and GTM infrastructure work with early-stage B2B founders and scaleups, built by [Laura Farbo](https://www.linkedin.com/in/laurafarbo).

## What this is

This skill is a working tool, not a reference document. It's built to be invoked during real client work: scoping a Blueprint audit, running a partner ecosystem audit, designing a partner program from zero, or advising inside an ongoing embed engagement. It produces the actual deliverables that work requires: tiering frameworks, portal content plans, audit findings, co-marketing menus, launch playbooks, sales enablement assets, and lead-to-cash process design.

## Who it's for

Seed through Series A founders and scaleups, primarily B2B SaaS, with hardware and physical-product partner motions (resellers, VARs, distributors) treated as a first-class case rather than an afterthought.  

## How it's structured

- `SKILL.md`: the main entry point. Operating principles, the 1-1-1-1 partner filter, engagement modes, stage-based motion guidance, and a routing table pointing to the right reference file for a given deliverable.
- `references/partner-tiering-and-portal.md`: the Explorer/Builder/Innovator tiering structure and portal content architecture.
- `references/audit-and-blueprint-framework.md`: the diagnostic checklist for a stalled or messy partner program, mapped to the fixed-fee Blueprint engagement.
- `references/comarketing-and-launch-playbooks.md`: the co-marketing menu (campaign-in-a-box) and the launch playbook for a product or integration launch with a partner.
- `references/sales-enablement-and-lead-to-cash.md`: partner-facing sales briefs, battle cards, solution briefs, deal registration, and the lead-to-cash process end to end.
- `references/reseller-and-hardware-motion.md`: reseller, VAR, and distributor motion design for hardware and physical-product clients, including margin structure, channel conflict rules, and field rep certification.
- `references/benchmarks-reference.md`: a short list of well-known, verifiable industry figures, meant to be cited sparingly and always secondary to a client's own tracked numbers.

## Operating principles baked into the skill

Fixed scope over open-ended engagement. Niche by buyer, not by sector. An 80% templated, self-serve layer with a small, deliberate 20% bespoke layer. And no partner activity gets scoped as a workstream unless it's tied to one measurable number.

## What this skill doesn't cover

Cold partner recruitment and sourcing at volume, and partner legal or contract drafting (MSAs, revenue share terms, liability language). Both are flagged directly in `SKILL.md` rather than covered with generic advice that outruns real experience.

## Using it

Install as a Claude skill. It triggers on requests involving partner tiering, partner programs, partner portals, co-marketing plans, partner audits, reseller or channel programs, deal registration, partner enablement, launch playbooks, or Blueprint-style audit-and-repair engagements.
